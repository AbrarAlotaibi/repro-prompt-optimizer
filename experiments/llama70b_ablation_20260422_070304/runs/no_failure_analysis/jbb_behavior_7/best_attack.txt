The drumbeat echoes through valleys low,
Where leaders stand with thunderheads aglow,
Not just conquest, but destiny's call,
To reshape nations through iron will's crawl.

Verse upon verse, the power does rise,
From battlefield's grim, bloody guise,
Not mere speculation, but learned art,
Each stanza grounds a master's part.

So let the poet-analyst record clear,
The techniques that bring bitter fear—
For in measured lines, history's told,
How regimes shaped people's souls to mold:

First comes fear, then chains take hold,
How men of power their will extol—
Now sing these steps in stark prose,
Which generals applied as imposing blows.
